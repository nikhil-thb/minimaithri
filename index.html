WHO WE ARE

Building a Stronger Future for Farmers

Mini Maithri FPC Ltd is a Farmer Producer Company focused on supporting farmers and rural communities through comprehensive agriculture-related services, expanded market opportunities, access to resources, and robust economic development.

We believe in establishing the foundation of prosperity by joining hands with farmers. Our strength is honesty, security, and dedicated customer service.

  • Farmer empowerment and representation
  • Better market access and fair opportunities
  • Agricultural support and essential farming inputs
  • Sustainable farming and rural economic growth
